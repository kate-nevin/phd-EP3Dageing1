18/03/2026 - Version 3

INDIVIDUAL CALIBRATION VIDEOS (checkerboard visible at all times):

	1) First take, focus only on Camera 1. You can move the checkerboard up and down so that It is always visible in its entire surface from camera 1 for the whole take. Then save the video

	2) Second take, focus only on camera 2. Move the chessboard so that its entire surface is visible to camera 2 for the whole take. Then save that video and do the same for camera 3. 

	3) Third take, focus only on camera 3. Move the chessboard so that its entire surface is visible to camera 3 for the whole take. Then save that video.

	In this way, you obtain 3 different videos from the cameras in 3 different takes, where the chessboard is visible only from one camera.

	The video does not require to be on the same take in this case, but it should be on 3 different takes for the 3 different cameras so that each camera sees the checkerboard in its entire surface for the whole time. You can move these 3 videos into a unique folder.

PAIRED_CALIBRATION:

For the Paired cameras videos:

	1) Take one: Focus on cameras 2 and 1, and move the chessboard so that it is visible for its entire time by camera 2 and 1. Then save the videos and put the separate video form camera 1 and 2 in a folder called “camera_2-camera_1”

	2)  Take two: Focus on cameras 2 and 3, and move the chessboard so that it is visible for its entire time by camera 2 and 3. Then save the videos and put the separate video form camera 1 and 2 in a folder called “camera_2-camera_3”

	3)  Take Three: Focus on cameras 3 and 1, and move the chessboard so that it is visible for its entire time by camera 3 and 1. Then save the videos and put the separate video form camera 1 and 2 in a folder called “camera_3-camera_1”

	The video does not require to be on the same take in this case, but it should be on 3 different takes for the 3 different pairs of cameras so that each pair of cameras sees the checkerboard in its entire surface for the whole time.
